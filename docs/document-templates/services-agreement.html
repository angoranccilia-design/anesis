Direct-Revenue Recovery Mandate

Services Agreement

Anesis Acquisition Ltd & Wren & Wold House · United Kingdom
The parties
This Agreement is made between Anesis Acquisition Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (the "Firm"), and Wren & Wold House of The Cotswolds, United Kingdom (the "Client"). It follows the Firm's Acquisition Thesis for the Client and sets out the terms on which the Firm will recover direct-booking revenue for the Client.
1

Definitions

"Direct Revenue" means room and ancillary revenue booked through the Client's own channels without payment of platform commission. "Baseline" means the agreed measure of Direct Revenue before commencement. "Recovered Revenue" means Direct Revenue in excess of the Baseline attributable to the Services. "OTA" means an online travel agent or booking platform.

2

Term

This Agreement runs for a fixed term of six (6) months from the Commencement Date, as selected by the Client and recorded in Schedule 1. It may be renewed by written agreement. The performance incentive for a six-month term is 15% of Recovered Revenue (a twelve-month term carries 10%); the rate follows the term, not the formula.

3

Services & deliverables

The Firm will act with reasonable skill and care, keep the Client informed, and provide the deliverables of the formula recorded in Schedule 1, with the objective of recovering Direct Revenue. For the formula recorded in Schedule 1 (Domination), the deliverables are precisely:

  1. Direct booking channel — rebuild and upkeep of the booking path, website conversion work, and retargeting with abandoned-booking recovery.
  2. Google Business Profile — full management.
  3. Reviews — monitored and answered across Google, Booking.com and TripAdvisor.
  4. Rate parity — full weekly enforcement across all connected OTAs.
  5. Conversion video & content — full-day production sessions per Schedule 1 (each yielding approximately 25 edited photos and 8 short-form videos, with model or influencer sourcing where relevant), plus a rolling 30-day content calendar of 16 posts per month across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Session count follows the mandate length (two for six months, four for twelve); output per session is set by the formula.
  6. Social channels — Instagram, Facebook and TikTok: content produced, published and community-managed.
  7. Paid acquisition — Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) and Google Ads (Search and Performance Max, as a dedicated channel).
  8. Email — monthly newsletter to past guests and automated nurturing & recovery sequences.
  9. Monthly recovery report — signed, per clause 6.
  10. Domination only — a named founder contact, area exclusivity (the Firm will take no competing hotel within the area agreed in Schedule 1) and priority turnaround.

The formulas differ, and Schedule 1 governs. The Growth formula limits social to Instagram and Facebook only, includes the monthly newsletter but not the email nurturing sequences, and excludes paid acquisition (Meta & Google Ads), full parity enforcement, the named founder contact and area exclusivity; rate parity is monitored, not enforced. Only the formula recorded in Schedule 1 is provided.

Video & photo sessions. The first session takes place in the second month of the mandate, allowing time to organise each shoot — including sourcing models or influencers where relevant. A six-month mandate includes two sessions (months 2 and 4); a twelve-month mandate includes four (months 2, 5, 8 and 10).

4

Client obligations

  1. Grant and maintain the access set out in Schedule 2 (booking engine, Google Business Profile, review platforms, social and advertising accounts).
  2. Respond to approval requests within three (3) working days, so the Services are not delayed.
  3. Provide accurate information and not knowingly undermine rate parity on its own channels.
5

Fees & payment

  1. The monthly fee in Schedule 1 is payable monthly in advance.
  2. Invoicing. The invoice for the coming month is issued approximately one week before the end of the current month and is due by the last day of that month.
  3. Non-payment. If an invoice is not paid by month end, the Firm suspends all Services from the 1st of the following month until payment is received. This strict measure exists solely to protect the continuity and quality of the Services.
  4. The performance incentive in Schedule 1 is a share of Recovered Revenue, invoiced once at the end of the term.
  5. Advertising spend is billed at cost with no mark-up, and only with the Client's prior approval of budget.
  6. Fees are exclusive of VAT, which is added where applicable.

6

Measurement & reporting

The Baseline is agreed in writing before commencement. Each month the Firm provides a signed report stating Recovered Revenue in pounds, the work performed, and the results by channel. The Client may inspect the underlying data on request.

7

Performance incentive & anti-avoidance

  1. The incentive is the percentage in Schedule 1 of Recovered Revenue, measured against the Baseline over the term from the Client's booking engine, analytics and channel data made available under Schedule 2. It is calculated on Recovered Revenue only — never on advertising spend or gross turnover — and is payable within 14 days of the end-of-term invoice.
  2. No diversion. The Client shall not conceal, under-report, cancel-and-rebook, or divert direct bookings outside the tracked channels — including taking bookings offline, through personal or undisclosed accounts, or through a substitute booking system — where the purpose or effect is to reduce the incentive. Any bookings so diverted are treated as Recovered Revenue for the calculation.
  3. Continuity of access. The Client shall maintain the Schedule 2 access throughout the term. If the Client withdraws access, obstructs measurement, or terminates other than for the Firm's material breach, the incentive is calculated on the recovery achieved to that date and extrapolated over the remainder of the term at the then-current monthly run-rate, and becomes immediately due.
  4. Tail bookings. Direct bookings generated by the Firm's work during the term but paying or checking in within 60 days after it count towards Recovered Revenue.
  5. Agreed measure. The Firm's measurement from the agreed data sources is the reference figure. The Client may inspect the underlying data, but any dispute must be raised in writing, with evidence, within 14 days of the relevant report; failing which the figure is deemed accepted.
  6. Late payment. Overdue incentive and fees bear interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and the Firm may suspend Services under clause 5 until paid.
8

Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only to perform this Agreement. This clause survives termination.

9

Intellectual property & data

Content and creative produced for the Client (video, copy, imagery) becomes the Client's property on payment of the relevant fees. Until those fees are paid in full, that content remains the Firm's property and the Client has no licence to publish or use it. Accounts, profiles and audiences remain the Client's throughout. Each party complies with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; the Firm processes personal data only to deliver the Services.

10

Warranties & liability

The Firm does not guarantee a specific revenue figure; its obligation is to perform the Services with reasonable skill and care. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the three months before the claim. Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

11

Termination & performance break

  1. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice after the initial term, or immediately if the other commits a material breach not remedied within 14 days.
  2. Performance break after three months. After the first three (3) months, if the performance objectives agreed in writing at commencement (Schedule 1) are demonstrably not met, the Client may terminate on 14 days' written notice, giving evidence against those objectives. In that case the performance incentive is calculated pro-rata on the Recovered Revenue actually achieved to the termination date, and fees are settled to that date only.
  3. This break applies only where under-performance is evidenced against the agreed objectives. It may not be used for unjustifiable reasons — including where the objectives have in fact been met, or where the shortfall results from the Client's own breach or withholding of access — in which cases clause 7(3) (run-rate incentive) applies instead.
  4. On any termination, fees due to the termination date (including any earned or pro-rata incentive) become payable, and Schedule 2 access is returned to the Client.
12

Commencement & governing law

This Agreement, and any obligation to pay, takes effect only from the Firm's official UK installation (the "Commencement Date"). It is governed by the laws of England & Wales, whose courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
