About Anesis Acquisition

A new firm, in a new market — built on a founder's real track record.

Where we come from

From KAIROS to Anesis.

Our founder built her track record under the name KAIROS, working with independent hospitality clients across Canada and French-speaking markets — real results, for real clients: a 22x return on advertising spend for a glamping operator, +372% Instagram growth for a spa, +156% direct bookings for a hammam & spa. Those results don't disappear. They travel with her as personal experience — presented separately from the new company.

Anesis Acquisition Ltd is a new entity, registered in the United Kingdom, that has not yet traded. That distinction matters for two reasons: our underwriting engine — the product itself — did not exist under the previous practice, and this is a new company built for a new market, not a rebrand carrying over an old trading history.

Why the United Kingdom, not North America

The problem is simply worth more here.

  • Platform dependency runs structurally deeper in UK independent hospitality than in North America — the leakage we price is larger.
  • Our model requires a physical presence — photo and video production, owners met in person — and the UK's geographic density makes that viable where a similarly-served Canadian market would not.
  • Independent UK hospitality trades year-round, supporting a full-time team, unlike a seasonal Atlantic Canadian market.
  • The UK is a gateway into the whole of Europe, where North America opens onto a single market dominated by large chains.
